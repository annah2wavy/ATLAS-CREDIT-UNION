About Our Bank


Atlas Credit Union stands as a trusted financial partner dedicated to empowering its members with stability and growth. Rooted in the values of community and accessibility, Atlas offers a full suite of services ranging from personal savings and loans to small business financing. With a name that evokes strength and global vision, the institution prides itself on combining traditional banking reliability with modern innovation, ensuring that every member feels supported in their financial journey.

Beyond everyday banking, Atlas Credit Union specializes in guiding members toward long‑term prosperity through wealth management, financial education, and digital solutions tailored to modern needs. Whether it’s helping families secure their first home, supporting entrepreneurs with startup capital, or offering cutting‑edge mobile banking tools, Atlas is committed to building financial confidence. Its mission is simple yet powerful: to carry the weight of financial responsibility with its members, providing strength in every transaction.
